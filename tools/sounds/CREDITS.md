# Qeyf sounds

- **Made for Qeyf** (`render_sounds.py`): the synthesized options (Kabarcık, Pıt, Qeyf, Hicaz, …), from the recipes of
  `qeyf-sesleri.html`.
- **Qeyf zili** (`snd_ring_plucky`): a 16-bar loop cut from the owner's own generated track (`cut_loop.py`).
- **Kenney, "Interface Sounds"** (www.kenney.nl), CC0 1.0 (public domain), via `import_kenney.py`:
  switch_006 (send, Anahtar), switch_001 (receive, Çıt), question_001 (notification, Soru), drop_003 (recording
  started), drop_004 (voice message sent), maximize_008 (call connected), minimize_008 (call ended).
  Credit is not required by the licence; it is given here with thanks.
